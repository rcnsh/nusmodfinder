# p2's report (screen-reader tree only, Sem 2, 2nd-year Computer Science), verbatim

I ran the p2 session (blind 2nd-year CS exchange student, Semester 2 only) entirely by keyboard and the accessibility tree. Of the four list courses, CS3244 is a clear yes, CS3240 is uncertain, and CS2106 and CS3231 are no. I picked two replacements, CS3234 and CS3210. Answers are in `answers.json` (6 entries) and notes in `notes.md`.

**Searches, in order** (setup on the home page: Sem 2, 2nd year)
1. `CS3244 CS2106 CS3231 CS3240`
2. `operating systems`
3. `automata formal languages computability`
4. `processes threads concurrency systems programming`

To read CS2106 and CS3231 in full I also removed and later restored the "Exchange students" and "Sem 2" filters.

**Answers**

| Course | Answer | What decided it |

I opened CS3211 Concurrent Programming and dropped it because it lists CS2106 as a prerequisite, the very OS course I can't take. None of the exam dates I picked clash.

**Problems found on the site**
1. **CS3240 gets two opposite answers.** With the exchange filter on, it shows as "Sem 2 · Open, with 1 note". With the filter off, the site says "CS3240 is hidden by your filters: it isn't offered in Sem 2." Meanwhile its result line says "Taught in Sem 1". A student can't tell from this whether it runs in Sem 2.
2. **Ticking the exchange box again resets my semester.** After I removed the Sem 2 and exchange filters and re-ticked "Only courses open to exchange students", the semester came back as "Both", not Sem 2. Nothing announced the change; I only found it by reading the panel.
3. **The exchange settings disappear when the filter is removed.** The semester radios and year selector vanish from "Your exchange", and a separate "Semester" combobox shows up under Filters. To a screen reader user the settings simply stop being where they were.
4. **"operating systems" finds no operating systems course.** The top hit was DAO2703 Operations and Technology Management, a business course, followed by electrical, engineering and other business courses. Nothing points to a systems alternative when CS2106 is closed.
5. **The "pinned codes" note is wrong.** It says "Only the first three course codes you type … are pinned", but with filters off all four of my codes were pinned.
6. **The year-by-semester table is very long by ear.** For CS2106 and CS3231 it has 5 years × 2 semesters, each cell repeating the same sentence, and each row's accessible name reads the whole row out. Every row is identical, so one sentence would do.
7. **Preclusion text is missing spaces.** CS2106 reads "Undergraduate DegreeTHEN(" and "at least DOR(must not be…", which a screen reader reads as garbled words.
8. **No help with prerequisites.** Prerequisites are given only as NUS codes ("As NUS states it"). Nothing tells an exchange student how home-university courses count. The CS3211-needs-CS2106 trap is only visible if you read that block.
9. **Nothing moves after a search.** Focus stays in the search box (its name changes from "What do you want to study?" to "Search courses"). Only the status message reports the results, so you have to Tab past the filter-chip buttons to reach them.

Things that worked: the skip link, the radios and comboboxes, the status messages, and the expand/collapse course buttons (expanded state is announced and "Close details" returns focus). The "Codes you typed that aren't shown" region with its reasons was clear and useful.
