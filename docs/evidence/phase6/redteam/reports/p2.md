**Problems I found:**
1. **The shortlist disappears when you type an address.** I typed a search URL into the address bar and the page showed "No courses yet". It did offer "Restore your last shortlist", which brought it back.
2. **The shortlist heading and copied link say "Sem 2".** The heading reads "Sem 2 · 3rd-year exchange student" and the link carries `sem=2`, even though the list covers both semesters. They just reflect whichever semester filter was last set. My advisor would open the link in a Sem 2 view.
3. **The PDF puts Semester 1 first.** My top choice, "Primary pick 1" (ST4234), only appears on page 3, after picks 5–8. The on-screen list is in my ranked order.
4. **The "Also as ST3236" link is easy to hit by mistake.** It sits between the card's Shortlist button and the details' Shortlist button. While tabbing I opened it by accident and ended up shortlisting ST3236 instead of MA3238. It's the same course, but it was unexpected.
5. **The "Both semesters" chip can't be used.** When "Both" is set, that chip can't be focused and has no remove ×, unlike the "Sem 1" and "Sem 2" chips. To switch semesters I had to open the Filters dialog every time.
6. **Ctrl+A doesn't select the search text.** I typed a new search and it was appended to the old one. Cmd+A worked.
7. **A stale source.** The guidance on Semester 2 choices cites a 2018/19 NUS info sheet. It's useful, but old.
8. **4000-level courses are shown as "Open" with a warning underneath.** NUS's note says they are "usually NOT available to exchange students". Seeing "Open" next to "usually NOT available" is confusing when ranking a 4000-level course as my top pick.
9. **The phone PDF hint was outdated.** The page says "On a phone: Share, then Print, then pinch out the preview", but the button saved the PDF directly.
