**2. Department and level**
- MA3210 and MA4251 are Mathematics; the other eight are Statistics and Data Science (both in the Faculty of Science).
- ST2137 is level 2000. MA3210, ST3131, ST3236, ST3248 and ST3247 are level 3000. ST4234, MA4251, ST4253 and ST4248 are level 4000.
- The exact level comes from the link. The PDF only gives a band: "Level 1000–3000, undergraduate" or "Level 4000, Honours".

**3. Units and study time** (from both)
- Every course is 4 units and about 10 hours a week.
- Each semester counts 16 units from the four primary picks, about 40 hours a week. Every course is marked "Counted, if NUS accepts you into it".
- Nothing is marked as units to confirm with NUS or units that count only if invited. The backup's 4 units aren't in the 16, but NUS's form lists 20 units per semester.
- NUS needs at least 12 units in a semester to admit the student. The maximum is 20 or 23 depending on which NUS source you read, so the student should ask NUS before planning more than 20.

**4. What each course covers:** there's one sentence per course in the JSON. The descriptions, prerequisites and preclusions are only in the PDF.

**5. Is each course open to the student?** Every course is "yes if"; none is a plain yes or no.
- All 10 are marked "Open to exchange students" (both sources).
- All 10 have prerequisites that depend on what the student has already taken (PDF only).
- Some prerequisites are met by other courses on this list:

  - ST4248 needs both ST3131 and ST3248, also Sem 1 picks.

**6. Which year:** the list rests on NUS's 2026/27 courses, its exchange course list dated 1 Sep 2026, and its 2026/27 exams (21–28 Nov 2026 and 26 Apr–4 May 2027). This comes from both sources.
