**Rules for changing courses in CourseReg** (the shortlist's "Already admitted?" section):
- Keep at least 12 units or three courses.
- Drop any course that clashes; no timetable clashes are allowed.
- Business, Law and Economics courses can't be added in CourseReg unless you got them at offer.
- Missing the drop deadlines can put a W or F on your transcript.
- A course not approved at application can be requested via "Submit Course Requests" or appealed as "Unable to secure course".
- You can request up to 23 units in one course request; above that you need a Max Workload Waiver.
- Courses not on the exchange list "may be available during Course registration": check the Vacancy Report.
- Get your home university's approval before changing your study plan.
- The Non-Graduating Office signs the Learning Agreement, and "no particular course can be guaranteed".
