TOTALS
Sem 2 picks

16 units counted, if NUS accepts you into each
16 units is about 40 hours a week at NUS's rate (2.5 hours a week per unit).

To be admitted, NUS must accept you into at least 12 units in a semester (NUS's brochure; GRO: “three pre-registered courses (or 12 units)”)
In CourseReg you must keep at least 12 units or three courses (GRO)
NUS’s brochure says at most 23 units a semester, but NUS Global’s exchange page says 20 (NUS Business School’s exchange students: 20). Ask NUS before planning more than 20. This limits the courses NUS enrols you in, not the courses you list.
NUS states hours a week only; your university's own conversion decides credit.
GRO: “Each semester normally includes 13 weeks of instruction and 2 weeks of examinations”
NUS'S APPLICATION FORM ORDER, FOR APPLICANTS

This order uses NUS's 2026/27 exchange list and exams. Applying for 2027/28? NUS publishes Sem 1's list in March or April, Sem 2's later (the 2026/27 list is dated 1 Sep 2026), and the timetable in mid-July: check each course there.

NUS's form asks, for each course with a prerequisite, the home course that meets it (NUS's application guide)

Already admitted? NUS's rules for CourseReg
Keep at least 12 units or three courses a semester (GRO)
Drop any course that clashes (GRO)
On Business, Law and Economics courses: “If you are not successful in gaining placements into these popular courses at the point of offer, you are not able to add these courses during CourseReg” (GRO)
Missing the drop deadlines “could lead to a Grade 'W' … or 'F' printed on your transcript” (GRO)
The Registrar's Non-Graduating Office signs your Learning Agreement or Study Plan (GRO)
“no particular course can be guaranteed by NUS” (GRO)
A course not approved at application can be requested in “Submit Course Requests” or appealed as “Unable to secure course” (NUS's CourseReg FAQ)
“Students are not allowed to read courses with any kind of timetable clashes” (NUS's CourseReg FAQ)
You can't drop below the minimum workload (NUS's CourseReg FAQ)
You may request “up to 23 Units in one course request”; above that, a Max Workload Waiver (NUS's CourseReg FAQ)
Courses not on the exchange course list “are not available during application stage but may be available during Course registration” (the Registrar's course restrictions, rule E)
“To check if the course is offered to Non-Graduating students, refer to the Vacancy Report” (NUS's CourseReg FAQ)
Get your home university's approval before changing your study plan (NUS's brochure)
