# 3H-0: the intent fields (2026-11-08)

3H-0 is the data half of plan step 3H. It stores the fields that the no-exam, S/U, workload and
prerequisite intents will filter on. It changes no ranking: on dev, all 98 lists of `3h0` equal
those of shipped `47838db`. The query side (3H-1) and L7 follow.

## What it stores
| column (migration 0007) | from | rule |
|---|---|---|
| `exam_semesters`, generated `exam_sem1..4` | `semesterData[].examDate` | offered semesters with an exam date |
| `su` | `attributes.su` | as given |
| `grading_basis` | `gradingBasisDescription` | as given. `su=1` will also admit CS/CU (C-12) |
| `workload_hours` | `workload` | the array's sum. NULL when missing, unparseable or all zero (C-11) |
| `prereq_none` | `prerequisite` | the field is absent (C-5) |
| `prereq_cohort_only` | `prerequisite` | only a cohort restriction, by the P-6 rule (A2-12, A3-7) |

- **Normaliser and row hash.** `normalise()` derives the fields. `NORMALISER_VERSION` is 2, so
  every `row_hash` changes, and ingest binds the fields.
- **The bundle filter row** carries them at bytes 20–31 under schema `vnm-vectors/2`. Verify's
  pre-deploy check compares them with D1.
- **The P-6 cohort-only rule** is mirrored into `data/cohort-only-prereq.json` by
  `scripts/build-3b-data.mjs`, as the collision list is. The eval workspace and the Worker can't
  read `eval/`, so normalise reads the rule through `src/cohort-only.js`. A test holds that
  reading equal to `eval/prereg/cohort-only.mjs`, and the review checked the two agree on all
  6,771 pinned prerequisites.

## Counts (critic C's, re-run: `node scripts/intent-counts.mjs`, `3h-0-counts.json`)
Every count matches the plan's §1:
- **Exams:** 35 modules have exams in some offered semesters and not others.
- **S/U and grading:** 933 modules have `attributes.su`, 648 are graded CS/CU, 1 is both, and 410
  are `grsu` (not stored; left to Phase 4).
- **Workload:**
  - 259 modules have an all-zero array, and 58 more have workload missing or unparseable, so 317
    are NULL;
  - 4,614 of 5,081 4-MC modules list exactly 10 h;
  - 379 modules are under 10 h ("light", 3H-1).
- **Prerequisites:** 4,180 modules have none and 65 are cohort-only, ACC1701XA and ACC1701XC
  among them. 4,245 are either.
