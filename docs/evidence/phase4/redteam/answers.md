# Questions put to the project owner, and the answers he chose (verbatim, 2026-11-10)

These are binding rulings. Where a source is ambiguous and a ruling below covers it, follow the ruling.

## 1. How strict should exchange mode be?
Question: "How strict should Phase 4's exchange mode be? SC1 says it must never show a course the student can't take in the chosen semester at their study level. The Registrar's restrictions PDF goes further than the NG list: some schools are closed outright (Medicine, Dentistry, Nursing), Law needs a Law major, Business a business major, many departments close level 4000 to NG undergraduates, and some courses are 'case by case'."

Answer: "NG list + restrictions PDF": "Hide what's closed: semester per the NG list, study level (UG: 1000–4000, with department 4000 rules; graduate: 5000+ only where the PDF allows it), closed schools and listed closed courses. Majors-only and case-by-case courses stay visible with a clear 'needs approval / major required' note. The rules become a reviewed, versioned data file, tested over the whole NG list."

## 2. Courses not on the NG course list (Q7)
Question: "Exchange mode, courses NOT on NUS's exchange (NG) course list: NUS's rule E says any other course 'may be approved case by case' at registration. Your earlier decision was to show case-by-case courses with a note, but that would add 5,476 courses (2,442 at levels 1000–4000) beside the 1,207 listed ones. Engineering (Civil, Industrial Systems) and Business say no to these either way. What should exchange mode do?"

Answer: "Hide them": "Exchange mode lists only what can be requested at application. The page explains that other courses may be approved at registration."

## 3. NG-listed courses a restriction rule seems to close (Q1)
Question: "About 25 courses are on NUS's exchange list but a restriction rule seems to close them. My defaults: (a) hide explicit closures (LL4011V, Medicine GEC1011/GEH1043/HSI2003, Logistics LI5202/LI5204); (b) blanket 'all CG / DSA / GEH codes' rules apply only to the department they're listed under, so those 10 courses stay open with a note; (c) level-5000 courses no department addresses: Architecture's dual-coded AR5221/AR5321/AR5601 shown as 'Architecture majors only', PC5202/5206/5211 open to graduate students only, CM5223/5225/5244/5268 and AR5801/5802 hidden. Accept?"

Answer: "Accept all defaults": "Use (a), (b) and (c) as written."

## 4. Four smaller defaults (Q2, Q3, Q5, Q6)
Question: "Q2: study level (1st–4th year undergrad, or graduate) is required to use exchange mode. Q3: the S/U filter keeps working, with 'graded basis only' notes where a department requires it. Q5: 36 courses where the exchange list and NUSMods disagree on semester are shown with a note. Q6: the 63 NUS College courses on the exchange list are treated as open, not restricted to partner universities. Accept all four?"

Answer: "Accept all four": "Use the four defaults as written."
